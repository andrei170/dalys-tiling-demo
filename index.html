What we do

Specialist commercial tiling, and the sectors we work in most.

Swimming pools

New installations and refurbishment. Pool tiling is its own discipline: movement joints, waterproofing, chemical resistance and a finish that has to survive constant immersion.

Care homes

Wet rooms, bathrooms and communal areas, worked around a live building with residents in it. Phased so a wing stays usable.

Leisure and changing rooms

High-traffic wet environments where slip rating and drainage detail matter more than the tile.

Commercial, general

Floors, walls, large format and screeding for contractors and facilities teams.

Why a commercial client picks a specialist

The things that decide a contract rather than a kitchen.

We price the whole scope

An itemised price for the full job, including preparation and waterproofing, rather than a day rate that makes the final figure a surprise.

We work to a programme

Start date, duration and sequence agreed in writing. On a care home or a leisure centre the date matters as much as the price.

We travel for the right job

We work up and down the country. For a job of the right size, distance is not the obstacle it is for a local tiler.
